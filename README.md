# 🐟 CritterClock

**Your Animal Crossing buddy that actually knows when to catch critters!**

Tired of casting your line 50 times just to catch another sea bass? CritterClock is here to help! The app analyzes spawn times and competing critters, it pinpoints the perfect moment to land the one you actually want.

## 🎯 What I'm Building

- **Smart Timing**: The app will tell you when the critter you're looking for is most likely to bite.
- **Less Competition**: The app figures out when fewer common critters are spawning, maximizing your chance to catch that rare critter.
- **All Your Favorite Games**: Starting with New Horizons, but there is plans to support more Animal Crossing games eventually.
- **Reliable Data**: The app use [Nookipedia's](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Main_Page) API for accurate and up-to-date information.
## 🚧 Heads Up!

**I'm still building this thing!** Right now it's design docs and caffeine, but i'm working to make your Animal Crossing life easier. The app is being built with React and Material-UI, with a visual design similar to New Horizons.

The current stable version can be viewed [here](https://ac-critter-clock.vercel.app/).
